## Full description (draft)

AlgoTrainer helps you build confidence in **algorithms** and **data structures** with structured lessons you can study on the go.

**What you get**

- Organised topics from core data structures to algorithms (sorting, searching, graphs, DP, and more).
- Readable explanations and **copy-to-clipboard** code snippets when you want to try things in your editor.
- Optional **account** so you can sign in and sync learning progress (see Privacy Policy for details).
- **Dark mode** and language preferences to match how you study.

**Privacy**

- We don’t use ads in this version. Account data is processed securely through Supabase.
- See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and how to delete your account.
